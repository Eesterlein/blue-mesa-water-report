How these numbers are made
Source
U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, Upper Colorado Region reservoir data for Blue Mesa (site 913): daily total release, storage and lake elevation. Copied on November 7, 2026.
Acre-foot
Enough water to cover one acre a foot deep, about 326,000 gallons. One cubic foot per second flowing for a full day is 1.98347 acre-feet, which is how daily release rates become yearly totals.
Normal year
The average of every full calendar year on record. "Normal for this date" compares January 1 through today's date in every year.
Full pool
7,519.4 feet above sea level. "Fullest ever" is the highest daily storage reading on record, which is lower than the design capacity because the lake has never been filled to the brim.
Left out
December 1968, the first month of records. Only years with a release reading on every day count toward the yearly records.
Downstream
Blue Mesa is the first of three Aspinall Unit dams on the Gunnison River. Its water flows into Morrow Point and then Crystal, so flows through the Black Canyon are measured below Crystal Dam.
